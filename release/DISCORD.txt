🍂❄️ **Seasons of Pywel 1.0 is out!** 🌸☀️

Four seasons for Crimson Desert that follow the in-game calendar and change **live** while you play.

🌸 **Spring**: fresh greens, some rain
☀️ **Summer**: hotter, rare rain
🍂 **Autumn**: golden and orange forests, frequent rain
❄️ **Winter**: snow on everything, real cold on the temperature meter, snowfall, and **deep snow that builds up while it snows** and melts after

• Weather and temperature follow the season, on the game's clock (sleeping moves it on)
• Control Menu on **F5**
• No game files changed. Install with DMM (ASI tab) or by hand
